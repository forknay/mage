## Licence quick-reference

Attribution requirements differ, and "free" on an asset site does not mean
"no obligations":

- **CC0 / public domain** — no attribution required. Still list it here so
  we know where it came from.
- **CC-BY** — attribution **required in the shipped build**, not just in
  this file. Anything CC-BY must also appear on the in-game credits screen.
- **CC-BY-SA** — viral. Avoid; it can force licence terms onto our own work.
- **CC-BY-NC** — non-commercial only. Fine for a private alpha, **a
  blocker if this ever sells**. Prefer to avoid it now rather than
  re-source everything later.

If an asset's licence is unclear, do not use it. There is always another
free footstep sound.
